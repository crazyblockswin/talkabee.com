Comments & Suggestions
How Talk A Bee Works

Learning a language should feel like an adventure. Here's how to get started:

🌐
Choose your languages

Select your native language, then choose the language you want to learn in a lesson or Adventure.

🍎
Learn the basics

Start with numbers, pictures, colors, expressions, and everyday words.

🧭
Explore Adventures

Visit exciting scenes, click objects to discover words, hear sounds, and try activities and challenges.

🔊
Listen, repeat, and enjoy

Hear spoken words, practice at your own pace, and return whenever you like.

No sign-up. No login. And it's FREE!

🐝 Editing Studio
Image added. Drag to position. Copy images/zero.png into the site images folder for saved HTML.
Drag an image while Edit is ON. Front places it above the category panel; Back places it behind. Server export keeps images and removes editing tools.

Draw a hotspot over your bee or any other part of the scene. Click it during normal play to hear its MP3.

Drag hotspots to move them, or drag the yellow bottom-right corner to resize. For saved HTML, copy the MP3 into your website sound folder. Choosing a file previews it immediately without embedding audio in HTML.
In Edit mode: drag inside a hotspot to move it; drag its bottom-right corner to resize it. Hotspots are invisible to visitors.
Save Working HTML keeps both editors. Save Server HTML keeps your images and sound hotspots but removes the editing tools.
