How Talk A Bee Works

Learning a language should feel like an adventure. Here's how to get started:

🌐
Choose your languages

Select your native language, then choose the language you want to learn in a lesson or Adventure.

🍎
Learn the basics

Start with numbers, pictures, colors, expressions, and everyday words.

🧭
Explore Adventures

Visit exciting scenes, click objects to discover words, hear sounds, and try activities and challenges.

🔊
Listen, repeat, and enjoy

Hear spoken words, practice at your own pace, and return whenever you like.

No sign-up. No login. And it's FREE!
