THE TALK A BEE IDEA

Don't memorize the trip. Take the trip.

A phrase is easier to remember when it belongs to a person, a place and a moment. So our lessons happen in cafés, markets, hotels, Grandma's kitchens and streets—not in empty boxes.

1
See itUse a beautiful scene and visual clue to understand the situation.
2
Hear itListen to the characters speak naturally, then slow it down.
3
Build itPut the sentence together so the structure starts making sense.
4
Say itTake a character's part and use the phrase yourself.
