Explore, Listen, and Learn

Across 54 interactive Adventures, Talk A Bee offers 991 original Activity and Challenge sentences, each available in all seven supported languages. That makes 6,937 sentence versions for visitors to explore.

Discover 945 interactive hotspots, listen to spoken words, and practice through Activities and Challenges at your own pace. All Activity and Challenge sentences are translated into the six additional languages.

The sentence totals cover Adventure Activities and Challenges. Hotspot labels, the Heaven Movie, and other learning pages are separate from those totals.
